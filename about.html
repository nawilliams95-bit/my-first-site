About RealtyDataLabs

Objective real estate intelligence for buyers, sellers, investors, and agents — always free.

Our Mission

RealtyDataLabs is an independent real estate intelligence platform built to give everyday people access to the same data that industry professionals use every day. Our mission is simple: aggregate the most important housing market data, mortgage rate information, economic indicators, and investment analytics into one clean, fast, and free platform.

We believe that objective data should be accessible to everyone — not just those who can afford expensive subscriptions or have insider connections. Whether you are a first-time buyer trying to understand if now is the right time to purchase, a seasoned investor analyzing cap rates, or an agent tracking national trends, RealtyDataLabs is built for you.

What We Provide

  • Market Data Aggregation — National home price indices, inventory reports, days on market, and sales volume data from the most trusted sources in real estate.
  • Mortgage Rate Tracking — Weekly Freddie Mac PMMS data, rate trend analysis, and financing news updated as soon as it publishes.
  • Economic Indicators — Federal Reserve policy decisions, CPI inflation data, employment reports, and consumer confidence metrics that directly move real estate markets.
  • Investment Analytics — Rental market data, cap rate trends, multifamily market reports, and investment strategy content for real estate investors at every level.
  • Regional Intelligence — City, metro, and state-level housing data so you can understand your local market, not just national averages.
  • Industry News — Real estate brokerage news, PropTech developments, NAR policy updates, and market commentary from leading industry voices.
  • Free Access, Always — No paywalls, no subscriptions, no accounts required. All data is freely available to anyone.

Who This Platform Is For

  • Homeowners who want to understand the value of their property and how market conditions affect their equity.
  • First-Time Buyers researching affordability, mortgage rates, and whether now is the right time to buy.
  • Home Sellers who want to price their home correctly and understand buyer demand in their market.
  • Real Estate Investors analyzing rental yields, cap rates, and market cycles to find their next deal.
  • Real Estate Agents and Brokers who need objective data to advise their clients and market themselves as informed professionals.
  • Researchers and Journalists who need reliable, sourced data for analysis and reporting.
