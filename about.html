About RealtyDataLabs

Objective real estate intelligence for buyers, sellers, investors, and agents — always free.

Our Mission

RealtyDataLabs is an independent real estate intelligence platform built to give everyday people access to the same data that industry professionals use every day. Our mission is simple: aggregate the most important housing market news, mortgage rate data, real estate investment intelligence, and media into one clean, fast, and free platform — covering Real Estate News, Rates & Market Data, Investment & Rental, and Media & Insights.

We believe that objective data should be accessible to everyone — not just those who can afford expensive subscriptions or have insider connections. Whether you are a first-time buyer trying to understand if now is the right time to purchase, a seasoned investor analyzing cap rates, or an agent tracking national trends, RealtyDataLabs is built for you.

What We Provide

  • Real Estate News — Breaking housing market news, home price trends, inventory data, and market analysis aggregated from Redfin, Zillow, and Realtor.com.
  • Rates & Market Data — Live 30-year and 15-year fixed mortgage rate data sourced from Freddie Mac via the Federal Reserve FRED API, plus CFPB and RISMedia updates.
  • Investment & Rental — Free investing guides, rental market data, and real estate investment strategies from FortuneBuilders, REtipster, and Keeping Current Matters.
  • Contact a Licensed Agent — Connect directly with Nick Williams, a licensed Georgia REALTOR® affiliated with KW Elevate, for buying, selling, or investing in North Georgia real estate.
  • Free Access, Always — No paywalls, no subscriptions, no accounts required.

Who This Platform Is For

  • Homeowners who want to understand the value of their property and how market conditions affect their equity.
  • First-Time Buyers researching affordability, mortgage rates, and whether now is the right time to buy.
  • Home Sellers who want to price their home correctly and understand buyer demand in their market.
  • Real Estate Investors analyzing rental yields, cap rates, and market cycles to find their next deal.
  • Real Estate Agents and Brokers who need objective data to advise their clients and market themselves as informed professionals.
  • Researchers and Journalists who need reliable, sourced data for analysis and reporting.
