Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RealtyDataLabs collects, uses, and protects information when you use our platform.

1. Who We Are

RealtyDataLabs (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is an independent real estate data aggregation platform operated by a licensed real estate agent in the State of Georgia. Our website is located at realtydatalabs.com. For questions about this policy, contact us through our Contact page.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact form and lead form submissions: When you submit the contact or lead form on our site, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and any information you include in the form fields (intent, location, timeline, price range, notes).
  • Newsletter sign-up: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.
  • Advertiser inquiry form: If you submit an advertiser inquiry, we collect your name, company name, email address, and inquiry details.
  • Analytics data: We collect non-personally identifiable information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and referring URL. This data is collected through analytics tools (see Section 5).
  • Cookies and local storage: We use browser cookies and localStorage to store session preferences and cached data (such as mortgage rate data) to improve your experience. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites.

We do not collect payment card information, Social Security numbers, or sensitive financial data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Lead form submissions are used to connect you with a licensed real estate agent who will follow up based on your stated needs.
  • Newsletter email addresses are used to send periodic market data updates and platform announcements. You may unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
  • Advertiser inquiry data is used to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential advertising arrangements.
  • Analytics data is used to understand how users interact with our platform so we can improve it.
  • Cookies and localStorage are used to cache data and improve page performance.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making, profiling, or to target you with advertising on other platforms.

4. We Never Sell Your Personal Data

RealtyDataLabs does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing or advertising purposes. We will never sell your data to data brokers, advertisers, or other third parties.

The only circumstances under which we may share your information with third parties are: (a) with your explicit consent; (b) to licensed real estate agents in connection with your lead form submission; (c) to service providers who assist in operating our website (such as our analytics provider), subject to confidentiality obligations; or (d) as required by law.

5. Analytics and Tracking Tools

We currently use Google Analytics 4 to understand traffic patterns and improve our platform. Data is processed by Google LLC under their privacy policy. Google Analytics 4 uses cookies to collect anonymized usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

We do not use advertising pixels, Facebook Pixel, or cross-site tracking technologies.

6. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. Used to maintain your browsing session.
  • Analytics cookies: Used by our analytics provider to collect anonymized data about site usage. These cookies do not identify you personally.
  • Preference cookies: Used to remember your preferences (such as sort order or filter selections) across visits.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party retargeting cookies. You can control cookie settings through your browser. Note that disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

7. Third-Party Advertisers and Affiliates

Our site contains affiliate links to third-party products and services (such as LendingTree, Bankrate, Better.com, PropStream, DealMachine, Buildium, DealCheck, Colibri Real Estate, BoldLeads, and others). When you click on these links, you may be tracked by those third-party platforms under their own privacy policies and cookie practices. We are not responsible for the data collection practices of these third-party platforms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

7a. Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google’s use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

Right to Restrict Processing

Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.

Right to Data Portability

Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.

Right to Object

Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.

Our legal basis for processing personal data from form submissions is your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using our Contact page.

9. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: You can request information about what personal data we collect, use, disclose, and sell (we do not sell your data).
  • Right to Delete: You can request that we delete personal information we have collected from you.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information. This right is therefore not applicable, but we acknowledge it.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise your California rights, submit a request through our Contact page with “CCPA Request” in the subject line.

10. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of any personal data we hold about you, please contact us through our Contact page with the subject line “Data Deletion Request.” Include the email address associated with your data. We will respond within 30 days and confirm deletion. Note that we may be required to retain certain records for legal compliance purposes.

11. Data Retention

We retain lead form and contact form submissions for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected (connecting you with an agent) and for any applicable legal retention period. Newsletter subscribers' email addresses are retained until unsubscription. Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of our analytics provider.

12. Children's Privacy

RealtyDataLabs is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

13. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal data we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page. Continued use of our platform after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

15. Contact for Privacy Requests

For all privacy-related requests, questions, or concerns, please use our Contact page and include “Privacy Request” in the notes field. We will respond within 30 days.
